Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Month Day, Year]

This Privacy Policy describes how BrainPulse Live ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. We've tried to write this in plain language rather than dense legal boilerplate, because the app's actual data practices are genuinely simple.

The short version: BrainPulse Live does not require an account, does not collect or transmit personal information to us, does not show third-party ads, and does not use tracking or analytics SDKs. Almost everything the app stores lives only on your own device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

BrainPulse Live does not:

  • Require you to create an account or sign in
  • Collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information
  • Access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or location
  • Use third-party advertising networks or ad-tracking SDKs
  • Use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services that transmit data off your device
  • Sell, rent, or share any information with third parties, because we do not collect information capable of being shared in the first place

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores a small amount of information locally on your device (using standard on-device storage — nothing is uploaded to us):

What's storedPurpose
Quiz/puzzle score, answer streak, and accuracy stats So the app can show your own progress across sessions
Which content items you've already seen So the endless content feed doesn't repeat items unnecessarily
Your configured content source URLs (if you've added any) So the app remembers where to fetch content from between launches
A cached copy of recently fetched content So the app still has something to show if you open it while offline or a content source is briefly unreachable
Your voice-guidance mute preference So wellness sessions remember your audio preference

None of this local data is transmitted to us, to the content source, or to any third party. It stays on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

3. Content Fetched From the Internet

The App displays quizzes, puzzles, and other content that it downloads from a content source you (or the app's default configuration) specify — typically a publicly hosted file on GitHub. This means:

  • The App makes standard HTTP requests to fetch that content, which means your device's IP address is visible to whoever hosts that content, the same as visiting any website
  • We do not control what a third-party content host logs about those requests; if you're concerned about this, review the privacy practices of whichever content source you've configured
  • If the App displays images alongside content, those images are also fetched from the internet the same way

4. On-Device Voice Guidance (Text-to-Speech)

Wellness sessions (breathing, eye exercise, desk stretch) include spoken guidance generated by your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. This processing happens entirely on your device — no audio, no transcript, and no usage data related to this feature is sent to us or to any third party.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction), because — as described above — the App does not collect personal information from any user regardless of age.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to how the App handles information, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Since the App does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly — please check this page periodically if you have concerns.
